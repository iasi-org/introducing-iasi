IASI
Engineering Assisted by Intelligent Systems

Introducing IASI

A way to rethink software engineering when intelligent systems become part of the real engineering process.

IASI01
Principle

Implementation is getting cheaper. Knowledge is becoming more valuable.

The model can produce code. Engineering still has to decide what should exist.

IASI moves the center of gravity toward explicit knowledge, decisions, constraints, validation and traceability.

The distinction
Generating an answer is not the same thing as engineering a system.
