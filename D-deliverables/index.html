Introducing IASI

Intelligent Assisted Software Engineering

Because when implementation costs less and less, knowledge becomes more and more valuable.

What is IASI?

Engineering for a world where intelligent systems are part of the process.

IASI is an open initiative exploring how software engineering changes when intelligent systems stop being occasional tools and become a real and natural part of engineering work.

It is not about replacing engineering. It is about rethinking how we define problems, preserve knowledge, make decisions, execute work and verify results.

The question is no longer how to add AI to the old process, but what engineering process this new reality requires.

IASI is the boundary

What comes from outside enters as input. Inside IASI it is understood, engineered and materialized. What leaves is output.

Inputs are organized by provenance: where the knowledge comes from and how it enters the engineering process.

externals

Material that originates outside IASI and is preserved as received.

internals

Knowledge and decisions created inside the IASI engineering process.

authored

The engine is a process, not a folder: understand the inputs, specify the result, plan the work, execute it, verify what was produced and authorize the result before it leaves IASI.

Inside IASI → Outside

Outputs and deliverables

Tasks materialize their results in outputs. Those results remain inside IASI until they are verified and authorized. Only an authorized output becomes a deliverable that can leave the system.

Delivery targets

Once an output has been verified and authorized, it becomes a deliverable and can leave IASI for its intended destination.

Human / Client Review, decision, handoff
Repository Source or artifact store
Deployment Runtime or environment
Publication Book, web, documentation
Communication Email, message, notification
Package Binary or distributable artifact

Deliverables leave IASI. Outputs do not.
